Zuoyan Zhang is a PhD student in the [CYCLE Lab](https://cyclelaboratory.github.io/) at [Hunan University](https://www.hnu.edu.cn/index.htm), supervised by Prof. [Jie Zhao](https://yaozhujia.github.io/). He is also a visiting PhD student at [Instituto Superior Técnico (IST), University of Lisbon](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/en/), working with Prof. [Nuno Lopes](https://web.ist.utl.pt/nuno.lopes/). He received his MEng degree from the State Key Laboratory of Mathematical Engineering and Advanced Computing at Information Engineering University in 2024, supervised by Prof. Shaozhong Guo and Assoc. Prof. Jinchen Xu.

His research interests lie in **Machine Learning Systems and AI Compilers**, with a focus on accelerating large language model training and inference. He explores automated parallelism, compute-communication co-optimization, and compiler optimizations to improve execution efficiency on distributed computing platforms. He is also interested in numerical program analysis, with prior work on floating-point error detection and automated program repair through expression rewriting. His research has been published in ASE, ISSTA, and ACM TACO. For more publications and research details, please see his [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=m6lSKEAAAAAJ) profile or the [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page.
